Project Scope

Design a salvage-vehicle auction platform end to end for a real client in the UAE — browsing and filtering an inventory of damaged and used cars, a vehicle detail page with a full condition report and bid history, a live real-time auction floor with countdown timers, a membership system that gates deposit amounts and bid limits by tier, an auction calendar, and checkout.

A note on context: this is client work — the consumer-facing browse-to-bid experience for a B2B vehicle bidding platform I designed alongside its data-heavy admin controls.

My role spanned the whole product: UX — the browse-to-bid flow, the condition-report hierarchy, and the membership gating logic; UI — every screen; and the design system — cards, status pills, countdown components and form patterns reused across the whole platform.

Problem Statement

Salvage-auction sites pack a lot onto one page — dozens of near-identical listings, each with specs, damage type, repair cost and a live bid, and a buyer has to open every single one to work out which cars are actually worth bidding on. That density is necessary, but most of these platforms present it as a wall of numbers with no clear read order, and membership rules (deposits, bid limits) are often buried until checkout.

Goal: make a data-dense marketplace scannable — surface the numbers that actually decide a bid (estimated value, repair cost, current bid) on every card, and make membership limits visible before a buyer commits to bidding.

Research & Ideation

I looked at how existing salvage-auction platforms lay out their listing and detail pages, and where buyers said they got stuck — mainly not being able to tell a car's real condition from its photos and title alone. Three decisions followed.

The same three numbers, every card — estimated market value, repair cost and current bid price sit on every listing card, in the same order, so a buyer can compare across the grid without opening anything. Condition is a score, not a paragraph — the vehicle detail page turns inspection notes into a Perfect/Imperfection score per system (engine, transmission, electrical, interior), so damage severity is scannable at a glance. Membership limits are visible before you bid — deposit and bid-limit tiers are laid out as a plain pricing table, not a rule you discover after trying to place a bid.

The core decision

Turn a condition report into a score you can scan in one second, not a paragraph you have to read.

Perfect / Imperfection scoring per system  ·  same three numbers on every card

Design & Results

The vehicle detail page leads with the essentials — title, current bid, a live countdown, and a "Bid Now" action — then a car overview score that splits condition into engine, transmission, electrical and interior, each scored out of 10 with a perfect/imperfection split, backed by a full auction history table and feature list underneath.

The live auction view keeps the same card language as browsing, but swaps the static price for a running countdown and a "Join Live Auction" action, so the transition from browsing to bidding feels continuous. A dedicated bid page adds bidding history, competing bids from other participants, and a large current-bid readout so a buyer always knows exactly where they stand. Membership is a flat four-tier pricing table (Free, Regular, Power User, Business) that states the deposit and bid limit for each tier up front, and an auction calendar shows every upcoming sale by date and location before a buyer even starts browsing.

  • Full product designed end to end — UX, UI and the design system.
  • Scannable listing cards — estimated value, repair cost and current bid on every card, in a fixed order.
  • Condition scoring system — inspection detail turned into a per-system Perfect/Imperfection score.
  • Live bidding flow — real-time countdowns, a dedicated bid page, and full bid history per lot.
  • Tiered membership — deposit and bid-limit rules stated up front as a plain pricing table.
  • 30+ screens across browsing, vehicle detail, live auction, membership, calendar and checkout.
