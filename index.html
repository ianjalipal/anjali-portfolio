About

Designing for the unglamorous middle of a product.

I work on the approval queues, review states and multi-role handoffs that make or break whether operational software actually gets adopted. My work spans clinic/EMR software, B2B trading platforms and marketplace apps — but the throughline is the same: take a process that's complicated because the business is complicated, and make the interface simple anyway.

I also work close to implementation — building in Claude Code, HTML/CSS, React and Storybook — so what I design is what actually ships.
