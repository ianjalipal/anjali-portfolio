Project Scope

Design JayHyzmat end to end — a native mobile marketplace for construction supplies in Turkmenistan. The build covers the full shopping lifecycle: sign-up and verification, a deep category browse, spec-first product pages, cart and a stepped checkout, order tracking, account management and in-app support.

My role spanned the whole product: UX — information architecture, user flows and wireframes; UI — every high-fidelity screen; and the design system — colour, type, spacing and radius tokens plus the component library the app is assembled from.

Problem Statement

Buying building materials in Turkmenistan means calling around suppliers one at a time — with no way to see who stocks what, at what price, or how far away it is. For trade buyers ordering by the pallet, distance and delivery matter as much as price: a cheaper valve two cities away isn't actually cheaper.

There was also no single, trusted place to compare products on real specifications — diameter, material, power rating, country of origin — the details a purchase actually turns on. Goal: put the local supply catalogue in one app, with the filters that decide a trade purchase built in — price, distance, brand and spec.

Research & Ideation

I mapped the catalogue structure with the team. Construction categories nest deep — Building Materials alone splits into Metal Doors, Fire Doors, Metal Railings, MDF Doors, Ceramic Slab Tiles, Laminate Parquet and Galvanized Sheet — so browse needed three levels: category → sub-category → filtered results.

Benchmarking large e-commerce and trade-supply apps surfaced three principles: buyers shop by spec, not by picture, so every product needs a full characteristics table; distance is a first-class filter, not an afterthought, because delivery reach decides the order; and trust comes from detail — verified reviews and clear availability counts let a buyer commit without a phone call.

The core decision

Spec-first, distance-aware — built for how trade buyers actually decide.

Full characteristics table on every product  ·  price, brand, diameter and distance filters

Design & Results

Every product page carries a full characteristics table and verified reviews, so a buyer can decide without calling the supplier. Browse runs category → sub-category → results, with a filter sheet covering price range, brand, diameter, colour and rating — plus the distance filter for delivery reach.

Checkout is a three-step stepper — Cart → Address → Order Summary — with saved addresses and a clear order-placed confirmation carrying an order number. The account area covers order history, addresses, password, notification and language preferences, and an in-app live chat for support.

Underneath sits a full design system: a green/orange palette with success, warning and error ramps; a mobile type scale; spacing and radius tokens; and a component library with every interactive state drawn — default / filled / error inputs, five bottom-navigation states, and empty states for cart and wishlist.
